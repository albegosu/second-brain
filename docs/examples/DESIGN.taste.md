# Taste

Generated by second-brain from the taste of 8 saved design captures (`python -m worker.design_md`), in the [DESIGN.md format](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md). Tokens are derived by code: measured palettes tuned to WCAG AA contrast, and the type, radius, spacing and depth the captures share. An explicit request or the project's own design system always wins.

## Overview

The look the owner keeps saving, across 8 design captures: geometric sans-serif type on a light background, barely rounded corners, airy spacing and flat surfaces, with smooth motion. It is the starting point when neither the user nor the project sets a style. The captures don't settle the accent hue, radius and motion feel: the tokens use the most frequent value, so follow the project where it has an opinion.

## Colors

- **Primary (#ff7a1a):** The main action on each screen, active states and key highlights. The most typical orange accent across the captures (no clear lead: orange 4 · red 3 · blue 2).
- **On primary (#16191d):** Text and icons on primary. 6.8:1 on primary. Picked for contrast on primary.
- **Secondary (#59616d):** Muted text: captions, metadata, placeholders and secondary icons. 5.8:1 on neutral. Adjusted from `#5b6470` for contrast.
- **Tertiary (#2f6fe4):** A second accent for tags, charts and secondary highlights, never the main action. The most typical blue accent, the most frequent hue that reads apart from primary.
- **On tertiary (#ffffff):** Text and icons on tertiary. 4.7:1 on tertiary. Picked for contrast on tertiary.
- **Neutral (#f4f6f8):** The page background. The most typical page color of the 6 captures with a light background (lead: light 6 of 8).
- **Surface (#d9dee5):** Cards, inputs, menus and other containers, one step from the page. Measured in the same capture as neutral.
- **On surface (#16191d):** Body text, headings and icons. 16.3:1 on neutral. Measured in the same capture as neutral.
- **Outline (#ccced1):** 1px borders and dividers. Derived: neutral, 18% of the way to on-surface.

## Typography

The captures' type is geometric sans-serif (lead: geometric-sans 3 of 8). DM Sans is a common open font of that family: a default, to swap for the project's own.

- **Headlines:** DM Sans 600, 24-48px, line height 1.1-1.3.
- **Body:** DM Sans 400, 14-18px, line height 1.6.
- **Labels:** DM Sans 500, 11-14px, for buttons, tabs, chips and metadata.
- **Fallback stacks:** `"DM Sans", "Avenir Next", Futura, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout

Spacing is airy (lead: airy 4 of 8): 24px inside controls and between related items, 40px inside cards, 64px between sections, 32px gutters and 64px page margins, with 8px and 12px for fine steps. Layouts across the captures: grid 1 · bento 1 (no clear lead). Density across the captures: balanced 2 (no clear lead).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is flat (lead: flat 5 of 8). No shadows: layers are told apart by the step from neutral to surface and by 1px outline borders.

## Shapes

Corners are barely rounded (no clear lead: subtle 3 · rounded 3): 4px on buttons and inputs, 6px on cards, 2px on chips.

## Components

- **Buttons:** primary is a primary fill with an on-primary label, 48px tall with 24px side padding and 4px corners; secondary is on-surface on surface, same size.
- **Cards:** surface on neutral with a 1px outline, 6px corners and 40px padding.
- **Inputs:** a neutral fill with a 1px outline, 48px tall, 4px corners, body-md text.
- **Chips:** tertiary with on-tertiary text, label-md.
- **Muted text:** secondary on neutral, body-sm, for captions and metadata.

## Motion

Motion is smooth (no clear lead: smooth 4 · snappy 3 · springy 1): unhurried, continuous transitions; nothing bounces. Easing across the captures: ease-out 1 · spring 1 (no clear lead). Animated properties across the captures: transform 2 · box-shadow 1 (no clear lead). Durations can't be measured from stills, so none is given.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do:** Keep text at 4.5:1 or more on its background: on-surface and secondary are already tuned to neutral and surface.
- **Do:** Keep primary for the one main action on a screen.
- **Do:** Follow the project on the accent hue, radius and motion feel: the captures don't settle them.
- **Don't:** Use primary for text or icons on neutral (2.4:1): only as a fill under on-primary.
- **Don't:** Add drop shadows: separate layers with surface and outline instead.
- **Don't:** Crowd: leave at least 64px between sections.
