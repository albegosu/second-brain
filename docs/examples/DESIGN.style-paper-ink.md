## Overview

Warm paper backgrounds, black ink and one hot accent, set like a printed page. A single narrow column of long text on warm paper, with oversized serif headlines and wide margins. Use it for: Essays, changelogs and long reads.

This is the topic's dominant look. It also holds: Cool Grotesk Variant. Each one has its own DESIGN.md: add `--look "<name>"` to the command above.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do:** Let the text column breathe with wide margins.
- **Do:** Use the accent for one thing per page.
- **Do:** Keep text at 4.5:1 or more on its background: on-surface and secondary are already tuned to neutral and surface.
- **Do:** Keep primary for the one main action on a screen.
- **Do:** Set headlines and reading text in the serif; keep buttons and labels in the sans.
- **Don't:** Add cards, shadows or rounded corners.
- **Don't:** Set body text in primary on neutral (3.7:1): use it as a fill under on-primary, or for large text and icons.
- **Don't:** Round corners: every radius is 0.
- **Don't:** Add drop shadows: separate layers with surface and outline instead.
- **Don't:** Crowd: leave at least 64px between sections.
